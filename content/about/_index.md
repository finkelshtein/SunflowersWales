        We are a group of friends whose members have organised fundraising events and regular shipments of humanitarian aid and medical supplies to Ukraine since 2014. Following Russia's full-scale invasion in February 2022, the need for reliable donations has become even more urgent.

        Our work is only possible because of your help and generosity. We work with trusted volunteers in Ukraine who promptly distribute our shipments to hospitals, medics near the front line, centres for displaced people and others in urgent need.

        Since April 2022, we have also brought Ukrainians in Wales together through cultural, educational and family activities, helping people build connections in their new communities while maintaining strong links with Ukrainian culture.
